## 1. Scope delivered

- **Sourced 1900 currencies as data.** `packages/data/data/historical/p3/currencies_1900.json`:
  every one of the 67 countries has an arrangement. 52 own currencies and the Maria Theresa
  thaler (foreign-issued, id 1000) are registered; 15 countries adopt another currency.
  Each own currency records its standard (gold, silver, paper), the metal of its legal
  unit, how its value is derived (Mint-table value, legal par, or fine content), its 1900
  circulating rate where paper circulated, its status and sources.
- **Derivation and validation.** `packages/data/src/currencies.ts` validates the file
  (named errors for unknown or missing countries, duplicate or reserved keys, unknown
  sources, non-positive or non-finite values, circular pars, paper without a rate or
  provisional status) and derives fine content, legal and circulating USD values and
  `rusdPerUnit = circulating USD × 392.6 / 25`.
- **`CurrencyValuation.rusdPerUnit` initialised from the parities** for the 1900 world
  (`source: "scenario"`); arrangements `own`/`adopted` from the table. The M5 sample keeps
  its unavailable valuations (D-080).
- **Month-end valuation history (schema 30).** `FinancePeriodSummary.closingValuations`
  records every specified valuation at each close (omitted when none is specified);
  validated, serialized and kept through compaction. Reporting-only.
- **Snapshot (protocol 19):** `CountrySnapshot.currencyId` and `monetaryArrangement`,
  `SimulationSnapshot.valuationHistory` (ledger-window months and annual means), next to
  the existing `currencies` with their valuations.
- **One formatter.** `packages/app/src/format/money.ts`; `useMoney()` in every screen with
  money; history at its month's or year's valuation; money sliders in display units;
  typed amounts converted to RUSD at the command boundary; worker display helpers take a
  required `MoneyText`; dispatch amounts formatted when shown.
- **Currency selector in Settings:** a Settings panel in the campaign sheet and a Settings
  entry (dialog) on the title screen. Own currency (default), RUSD, and every valued
  registered currency. Stored per device in AsyncStorage, never in a save or the world.
- **D-140** records the designer's requirements (gold and silver as mined, traded goods;
  fluctuating currencies; giving up or creating a currency) as a proposed model for P5,
  P6 and P11.

## 3. Behaviour and design decisions

- **Sources.**
  - Director of the Mint, *Values of Foreign Coins, January 1, 1901* (pp. 56–57, read from the page scans), for the legal unit value.
  - The same report's 1900 answers on actual currency, premium on gold and exchange on London, for paper.
  - Its monetary-systems chapter for the Latin Union pars and the tical.
  - The Gold Standard Act (1.504632 g fine gold per dollar).
  - Silver at the Mint's own Oct–Dec 1900 valuation: 0.6478 USD per troy ounce, from the Mexican dollar.
  - The derived fine contents reproduce the legal standards: pound 7.322 g (legal 7.32238), mark 0.358 g, ruble 0.775 g, dollar 1.5046 g.
- **Paper at the 1900 circulating rate:**

  | Greek drachma | 58 % gold premium | $0.122 | |

- **Adopted arrangements:**

  | Countries | Currency adopted |
  |---|---|
  | Montenegro, Liechtenstein | Austro-Hungarian crown |
  | Andorra, Monaco | French franc |
  | San Marino | Italian lira |
  | Australian colonies, New Zealand, Tonga, Orange Free State | Sterling |
  | Ethiopia, Oman, Wadai, Darfur, Ha'il, Sokoto | Maria Theresa thaler |

  Adopted countries have no monetary authority, so 90 fewer monetary accounts are allocated; later account ids shift by 90, and balances are identical.
- **History** converts at the valuation of its own closed month (the annual reports use the mean of that year's month-end valuations). It falls back to the current valuation when none was recorded.
- **Display only.** The preference is per device; the worker protocol has no display command. Money sliders use 1-2-5 steps in display units and report RUSD. Rates (valuations, parities) stay in RUSD per unit.

Acceptance criteria (spec):

1. **Every money label goes through the formatter.** Audit tests in `p3-local-currency` check that:
   - no RUSD-only formatter, hand-built RUSD text or `…RUSD.toFixed` appears in app code;
   - no slider is in an RUSD unit;
   - every file that formats money gets a formatter;
   - the worker helpers print no unit of their own, and a non-RUSD formatter reaches every helper's output.

   **Met** (static audit plus a runtime check of the helpers; screens are not rendered in tests).
2. **Display currency changes no world hash.**
   - A 1900 snapshot formatted in all 55 choices leaves the world hash and the snapshot unchanged.
   - 33 days run while switching currencies equal 33 days run without.
   - The protocol has no display command.
   - In the browser, switching does not advance the date.

   **Met.**
3. **Forced devaluation.** On the M5 sample with an active authority, halving the parity (0.5 → 0.25):
   - doubles the local treasury and local prices;
   - leaves the RUSD treasury, RUSD prices and another currency unchanged;
   - history keeps the old month's rate.

   **Met.**

## 6. Assumptions and provisional values

From D-141:
- **Paper discount not sourced, so the metal parity stands:** Italy, Chile, Brazil, Colombia, Bulgaria, Ecuador.
- **No 1900 value for the country's own unit, so a stand-in is used:**
  - Afghanistan and Nepal at the Indian rupee.
  - Dominican Republic at the Central American silver peso.
  - Korea at par with the yen.
  - Luxembourg at the franc.
  - Paraguay's gold reference at the Argentine gold peso.
- **Adoption choices:** Andorra (franc), Oman (thaler), Sokoto, Wadai, Darfur and Ha'il (thaler).
- **Secondary or OCR-read figures:** the thaler's fine content is secondary. The answers for Spain, Guatemala, Nicaragua, Salvador, Haiti, Liberia, Serbia, Paraguay and Austria-Hungary come from OCR text, not the page scans.
- **Not registered:** colonial currencies.
- **Fixed until D-140:** silver currencies keep their opening RUSD value, because metal prices are not simulated yet.
- **Rounding:** money sliders now step in display units, so a committed RUSD amount is a display-unit multiple × the valuation rather than a round RUSD number.
