# M9 — Technology and Education: requirements map

Status: implementation planning record for M9. It maps the authoritative GDD v2.13
M9 definition (§10.1–§10.3, §6.3, §6.8, §9.2, §17 milestone table, §17.5) to
concrete code paths, authoritative state, simulation behaviour, player actions,
and tests. It also records the focused M8 closure review. The GDD is authoritative;
this document does not invent scope beyond it, and the "deferred" column is part of
the scope decision recorded in D-077.

## 0. M8 closure review (focused, non-speculative)

Repository state at the start of M9: `main` at
`ea89a7ce41c4ccae1e5f9e40dd5fb52a03482e56`, clean working tree. That matches the
reported M8 final HEAD.

The M8 acceptance criteria are §9.1–§9.5 and the §17 M8 row ("law/election
outcomes follow documented prerequisites; a revolution or regime crisis can arise
from simulated causes; scripted events are idempotent and have provenance"). The
implementation record (§17C) and D-076 document:

- interest groups derived from pop blocks, non-spendable influence — implemented;
- laws validated at the worker/simulation boundary with prerequisites, regime
  eligibility, support, and effective-day timing — implemented;
- elections only under an elected regime with franchise, population, and a
  minimum institution quality — implemented;
- unrest/crisis/revolution from measured conditions, no hidden timer — implemented;
- institution quality/overload effects on tax collection, construction, enactment,
  and production during strikes — implemented;
- scripted events idempotent with provenance — implemented.

M8's deferred items (education/health/currency/colonial/military-service law
mechanics; tariffs/customs; minimum wages/bargaining/social insurance; AI law
proposals; crisis-response actions; riots) are consistent with D-076 and the GDD:
each is assigned to a later stage (M9/E5/E6b/M10) and no M8 acceptance criterion
requires it. The education slot in particular was explicitly deferred to M9.

**Conclusion:** no concrete mandatory M8 acceptance failure was found before M9.
No M8 remediation is needed. M9 must keep M8 behaviour intact and add education
law mechanics through the existing M8 law pathway rather than replacing it.

Explicitly **not** M9: E5a/E5b education/health *spending services*, training,

later stages may consume but implements no later-stage mechanic.

## 3. Authoritative state summary

- `World.technology.enabled` — scenario policy; `false` for legacy fixtures, so M9
  is inert there.
- `Country.technology` — researched technologies (ascending id, completed day),
  the single active project, and project-specific accumulated work.
- `Country.education` — annual funding target, research staff share, last update
  day, month-to-date requested/shortfall memo.
- `PopBlock.literacyByAge` — authoritative age-specific literacy; `literacy` remains
  the derived 15+ aggregate used by employment, politics, and AI observations.

Derived values (staffing, capacity, enrollment, attendance, quality, productivity
factor, mortality factor, education bonus) are recomputed from these inputs and the
validated definitions; they are never stored as a second authority.

## 4. Ordering and timing decisions

1. Demography reads the technology mortality factor as of the start of the day.
2. Production reads the sector productivity factor as of the start of the day and
   applies it to produced output.
3. The education/research phase runs after market settlement and the fiscal phase,
   before the industrial/business-tax/construction/debt phases, so the payroll is
   in the same accounting period and a technology completed today applies from the
   next civil day. This is deterministic and documented in D-077.
4. Education literacy changes apply once per processed day to the school-age
   cohort. Because the whole day is staged and published atomically, a retry cannot
   apply the change twice.
5. A technology completes when `workByTechnology[tech] >= researchWork` after the
   day's work; the researched record's `completedDay` is that day, and its effects
   are read from the next day forward. Historical periods are never rewritten.

## 5. Accounting summary

| Flow | Posting | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Education payroll | Country `education-expense` debit / treasury credit; academic household cash debit / `education-income` credit (`education-payroll`) | Cash-neutral transfer; actual treasury cash limits payment; shortfall is reported, not booked as debt |
| Research | no monetary posting | Real labour only; funded through the same education payroll |
| Technology completion / effects | no monetary posting | Real state/behaviour change only |

Fiscal totals add optional `educationExpenseSUSD`; budget totals add
`educationRequestedSUSD`/`educationShortfallSUSD` (sparse, optional, default zero).
