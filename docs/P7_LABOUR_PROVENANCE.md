## Provisional rows

| Id | Measure | Value | Reasoning |
|---|---|---:|---|
| farmer-to-laborer-wage | farm operators' reference wage relative to the production-worker wage | 1 | No 1900 farm-operator income series was found (the Douglas and Lebergott series cover employees). The wage only splits an owner-operator's income between wage and profit (plan §4.1); the residual reaches farmers through profit distribution. |
| shopkeeper-to-trade-wage | shopkeepers' reference wage relative to trade employees' earnings (D 752) | 1 | Proprietors are not in the employee series; trade employees' earnings stand in. Owner-operators earn the residual through profits. |
| serviceman-to-laborer-wage | career enlisted pay relative to the production-worker wage | 1 | As the P2b soldier pay (a soldier is paid as an unskilled worker); the army pay table of 1900 was not transcribed. Servicemen are not populated in P7 (plan Q2a). |
| officer-to-federal-wage | officers' pay relative to federal executive employees (D 790) | 1 | Officers are not populated in P7 (plan Q2a; counted in Bureaucrats). |
| medic-to-clerical-wage | medics' reference wage relative to clerical salaried staff (D 787) | 1 | D 756 ($256, 1901) covers health-service employees only; physicians, half of US medics in 1900 (Webb p.443: 132,000 physicians), were self-employed and earned more. Salaried clerical pay stands in until a physician income source is transcribed. |
| engineer-to-clerical-wage | engineers' reference wage relative to clerical salaried staff (D 787) | 1 | No 1900 engineers' salary series was transcribed; the salaried staff of manufacturing and railroads stand in. |
| academic-to-clerical-wage | academics' reference wage relative to clerical salaried staff (D 787) | 1 | Academics were priced at the teacher salary in P2b; teachers now have their own profession. Salaried pay stands in for professors, lawyers and writers. |
| aristocrat-to-capitalist-wage | aristocrats' reference wage relative to capitalists | 1 | Land income reaches aristocrats through profits; the wage only splits income. |
| engineer-share-heavy-industry | engineers' share of a heavy-industry or power building's non-manual jobs | 0.25 | Plan §3.4: provisional share of the clerks' slots of heavy industry and power buildings re-mapped to engineers. |
