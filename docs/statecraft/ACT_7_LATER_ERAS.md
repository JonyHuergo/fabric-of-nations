# Act VII — Later eras (S24 to S27)

After Release 3 the systems exist. This act is mostly **content on finished systems**: inventions,
methods, goods, laws, treaty terms and data for the rest of the hundred years, plus the few
mechanics that the later decades need and the earlier ones did not.

Each stage begins with a stage plan approved by the designer (handbook §5). The definition of done
of handbook §8 applies in full.

Read first: `HANDBOOK.md`, `CONTRACTS.md`, the whole program, and the completion reports of the
stages whose systems you extend.

## Rules for this act

1. **No era flags and no era rule sets.** A decade differs from another because its inventions,
   laws, institutions and stocks differ. If a later period seems to need a special rule, the
   earlier rule was too narrow: generalise it and re-run the earlier tests.
2. **Nothing is scheduled.** The depression, the second general war, decolonisation, the oil
   shocks and the end of the planned economies may arise from the systems. The Observatory
   reports how often and when. The designer sets any target in the stage plan, as for the Great
   War; a target is met by structure and dispositions, never by a dated event.
3. **Performance holds.** The number of countries grows several times with decolonisation. The
   day budget of program §4.5 still applies; the AI slice and the diplomacy pairs are the items to
   watch.
4. **Every addition goes through the same registries**: Desk cards, lenses, Gazette events,
   indicators, forecasts, AI use.

## S24 — From the 1920s to the 1940s

| Theme | What is added | Built on |
|---|---|---|
| Depression | Nothing new if S11 is right: a credit boom, a crash, bank failures and the constraint of gold. Check in the Observatory that severe depressions occur and end | S11 |
| The planned economy | Under state ownership, the government sets output targets and allocates investment by industry; prices are administered; shortages show as queues (the rationing rule of S22); state firms' losses are covered by the budget. Collectivisation is a land law | S10, S14, S22 |
| One-party regimes | Communist and fascist constitutions (S14 rules): a party as the body of consent, a funded press with wide reach, repression, purges (which also lower officer quality), a mandate that demands expansion or autarky | S14, S15 |
| Mechanised war | Tanks, trucks, radio and aircraft as inventions and equipment. The combat rule of S20 must now give mobile war; run its two-era fixture on the real data. Strategic bombing and carriers in full | S12, S20, S21 |
| Intelligence | Codebreaking and radar as inventions whose effect is information: narrower ranges, revealed preparations | S16, S21 |
| Mass society | Radio, the first welfare laws (pensions, unemployment insurance), women's suffrage | S14 |

Acceptance: Observatory runs from 1900 to 1945 report the incidence of depressions, the
distribution of regimes and of general wars; a planned-economy fixture shows fast growth of heavy
industry with consumer shortages; conservation and the day budget hold.

## S25 — From 1945 to 1970

| Theme | What is added | Built on |
|---|---|---|
| Nuclear weapons | A state programme (S12) yields warheads from real fissile material (deposits, reactors and enrichment plants). Use destroys the people and buildings of a province. In a crisis (S17) the expected cost of war includes it, so resolve falls between armed pairs. Arms-control terms | S12, S17, S16 |
| Blocs | Alliances with integrated commands and bases; aid as loans and grants; ideological kinship; support of factions in other states' civil wars with arms and advisers | S16, S15 |
| Decolonisation | National mobilisation (S13), a metropole weakened by war and debt, and pressure from the powers lead to independence by treaty or by war. Many new polities | S13, S15, S16 |
| International organisations | A treaty with members and voting rules that can sanction or authorise force; a system of fixed exchange rates as a monetary arrangement; multilateral commercial rounds | S16, S11 |
| Mass consumption | New goods through the Engel system (motor cars, appliances, television); high-yield farming methods; the welfare state as laws | S12, S14 |
| Space | State programmes whose return is prestige and knowledge | S12 |

Acceptance: a deterrence fixture (crises between nuclear-armed states end in war less often,
while proxy conflicts still occur); the Observatory reports new states per decade against a
historical band; the day stays in budget with about 150 countries.

## S26 — From 1970 to 2000

| Theme | What is added | Built on |
|---|---|---|
| Energy | Methods that depend on oil; concentrated deposits; an output agreement among producers as a treaty term. Price shocks then follow from the market | S12, S16 |
| Money | Floating currencies (the exchange-rate system exists), inflation and its cure, central-bank independence as a law, sovereign debt crises | S11 |
| Globalisation | Lower trade barriers, foreign ownership, container shipping as a method that lowers freight | S9, S10, S16 |
| Services and electronics | New goods and industries; computing inventions; the shift of employment out of industry | S12 |
| The end of planned economies | Stagnation erodes support; reform or collapse through S15 | S14, S15 |
| Integration | Customs and currency unions among states (both mechanisms exist) | S16, S11 |
| The end | The campaign closes on 1 January 2000 with the final review and the chronicle | S7, S6 |

Acceptance: hundred-year Observatory runs report the shares of employment in agriculture,
industry and services against historical bands (the fall of the first, the rise and relative fall
of the second, the rise of the third: GDD pillar 2), world product per head, world population in
2000, the number of states, and the day's cost in the last decade.

## S27 — Hundred-year calibration and release polish

- **Calibration** (the Prosperity Program's P15). No-input and AI-only runs of the full hundred
  years against Maddison's product per head, urbanisation, employment shares, life expectancy and
  fertility, with documented bands. A coefficient changes only after its structural cause is
  diagnosed (GDD §6.6). Coefficients that hold are promoted from provisional to calibrated, with
  the evidence.
- **AI and difficulty.** The league of builds on fixed seeds; the difficulty levels tested by
  what they change (foresight and error only).
- **Play.** Onboarding, the pitch and difficulty of each country, the pace of decisions across the
  eras, the era skins, achievements, seeded challenges.
- **Access.** Translation-ready strings checked, text scaling, colour-blind palettes.
- **Devices.** The designer's tests on phones and tablets for speed, memory and heat; the fixes
  they call for.

Not planned here: later start dates. Each needs a complete scenario (population, economy, firms,
forces, treaties) for its date. Propose it only if the designer asks.
