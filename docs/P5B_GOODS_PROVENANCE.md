## Hydro potential (C-12)

Province hydro potential (`historical/p5b/hydro_province_1900.json`, from `scripts/p5b/extract-hydro-riveratlas.mjs`) is derived from **HydroSHEDS RiverATLAS v1.0 (shapefile release)**: Linke, S., Lehner, B., Ouellet Dallaire, C., Ariwi, J., Grill, G., Anand, M., Beames, P., Burchard-Levine, V., Maxwell, S., Moidu, H., Tan, F., Thieme, M. (2019). Global hydro-environmental sub-basin and river reach characteristics at high spatial resolution. Scientific Data 6: 283. doi:10.1038/s41597-019-0300-6. Attributes: dis_m3_pyr: WaterGAP 2.2 (Döll, Kaspar & Lehner 2003, Journal of Hydrology 270: 105-134); ele_mt_cmn and sgr_dk_rav: EarthEnv-DEM90 (Robinson, Regetz & Guralnick 2014, ISPRS J. Photogramm. Remote Sens. 87: 57-67); HYRIV_ID, NEXT_DOWN, LENGTH_KM: HydroRIVERS (Lehner & Grill 2013, Hydrological Processes 27(15): 2171-2186). Source: https://www.hydrosheds.org/hydroatlas, RiverATLAS_Data_v10_shp.zip (figshare file 20087486, 2,418,581,202 bytes), accessed 2026-11-08. Licence: **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**; the derived table is an adaptation (aggregated to the game's 1900 provinces), and the raw data are not redistributed.

Method: gross theoretical potential, MWh/year: per reach, 1000 kg/m³ x 9.81 m/s² x dis_m3_pyr x (ele_mt_cmn - ele_mt_cmn of its NEXT_DOWN reach, both made non-increasing downstream) x 8,765.82 h / 10^6; a headwater reach adds 1000 x 9.81 x dis_m3_pyr/2 x sgr_dk_rav/10 x LENGTH_KM x 8,765.82 / 10^6; a reach goes to the province containing its middle vertex (the smaller on an overlap), else the nearest label within 0.5°. Unit: MWh/year (gross theoretical; the simulation applies HYDRO_TECHNICAL_SHARE). World gross potential 49.805 PWh/year (0.567 unplaced; 3.261 from headwater reaches).

- Gross theoretical potential at long-term mean flow: no seasonal flow, no technical, economic or environmental limits (the simulation applies one provisional technical share).
- Discharge is the naturalised 1971-2000 mean at each reach's downstream end; the 1900 climate and any upstream use are not modelled.
- Greenland is left out (no gradient data, and DEM outliers over the ice sheet).
- 1900 hydro stations generate 12,260 MWh a day of the 77,780 MWh placed (the 1902 US water-wheel share, within each province's cap).

The Hoes et al. (2017) site dataset (4TU.ResearchData) was not used: its General Terms of Use allow reuse only for non-commercial purposes (designer, 2026-11-08).
